About The Studio

Crafted by hands that understand machines

WRENCH was born from an obsession with automotive heritage. Founded in 2013, our workshop has grown from a two-bay garage to a 12,000 sq ft facility equipped with state-of-the-art tools and a team of 8 master technicians.

Every vehicle that enters WRENCH leaves as a testament to what's possible when craftsmanship meets unwavering attention to detail. We specialize in complete frame-off restorations, bespoke engine builds, and competition-ready custom fabrication.
